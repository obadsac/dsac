Legacy & Heritage

The Evolution of Our College

2000

The Humble Beginning

Established on February 1, 2000, at Mancholai Thaqwa Pallivasal with just 19 students, aiming to provide quality Islamic and secular education.

2009

A New Chapter

The college expanded and relocated to the Semmannodai Kuba Jummah Mosque with 35 incoming students, paving the way for rapid growth.

2013

Infrastructure Growth

A major milestone was reached on November 26, 2013, with the grand opening of the newly constructed three-story college building.

Now

Silver Jubilee

Today, celebrating 25 years, the college thrives with 250 students, well-equipped facilities, and diverse educational programs.

Hifz & Academics

Providing robust programs including Hifz, Al-Quran & As-Sunnah Academy, merging traditional and modern education.

Student Societies

Fostering talent through active student bodies like the Dharus Salam Art Club (Kala Manram) and Youth Forum.

Modern Facilities

Equipped with a modern library containing thousands of books and resources in Tamil, English, Arabic, and Sinhala.
