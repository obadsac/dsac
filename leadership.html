Past Administrations & Milestones

Term: 2022 - 2024

Led by: Dr. M.R. Kabeer

Key Achievements:

  • Successfully completed the construction of the New IT Laboratory (Cost: Rs 5.2M).
  • Introduced the 'Annual OBA Scholarship Fund' assisting 50 students.
  • Hosted the Grand Centenary Alumni Meet in Colombo.
Term: 2019 - 2021

Led by: Eng. A.L.M. Naleef

Key Achievements:

  • Spearheaded the COVID-19 Relief Fund, providing dry rations to 500+ families.
  • Renovated the School Main Pavilion and Sports Complex.
  • Launched the first digital OBA member database.
Term: 2016 - 2018

Led by: Mr. S.H. Rila

Key Achievements:

  • Donated a 54-seater School Bus for student transportation.
  • Organized the first inter-school OBA cricket tournament.
